# Supplier continuity and disruption response
Source: Synthetic operations playbook / DEMO-OPS-01

When a supplier becomes unavailable, the procurement planner should examine approved alternate suppliers for each stock-keeping unit (SKU), their delivery lead times, and total throughput capacity. A low quoted unit price is not sufficient evidence of reliable supply. For critical products, diversify allocations rather than relying on a single high-risk supplier. Escalate projected service-rate shortfalls to supply planning leadership.
