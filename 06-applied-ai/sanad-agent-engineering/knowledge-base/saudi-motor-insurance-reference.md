# Task 1 — Starter knowledge base (approved public facts, not a fictional insurer's operating manual)

**Important:** This is a seed KB for assessment testing, not a production-approved insurance policy. Verify current official documents and add the actual insurer's own claims, products, tools, channels, and branch directory before live use. In particular, no bank/policy/branch/claim-status data has been invented.

## 1. Public safety and official reporting
- Saudi government emergency directory: Unified emergency number **911**; ambulance **997**; traffic accidents **993**; Najm insured-vehicle incidents **920000560**; police **999**. Source: https://my.gov.sa/en/emergency-contact
- Tawuniya's publicly published insured-vehicle accident steps: check safety, use warning lights, call 997 for serious injuries, notify Najm via app or 920000560, document scene safely, wait for the authorized investigator, then submit the insurer's claim application. Source: https://www.tawuniya.com/en/individuals/car-accident
- Official Najm online reporting page: https://najm.sa/report-accident/ . Najm reporting must be limited to cases Najm is authorized to handle. If the situation is unsafe, involves injury, is outside coverage conditions, or is unclear, follow the relevant authority's instructions; never assume Najm accepts every accident.

## 2. Claim flow and distinction between services
- Basic sequence, where applicable: **ensure safety → notify competent authority → receive accident report/reference → obtain authorized damage assessment as required → submit documents to appropriate insurer → insurer reviews → claim decision/repair/settlement**.
- Taqdeer is a recognized vehicle damage assessment system; its operational assessment duration is not the insurer's claim settlement period. Website: https://taqdeer.taqeem.gov.sa/en
- Do not promise exact processing times for a particular user's assessment unless the applicable service and appointment details are available from a verified source.

## 3. Regulatory example: compulsory third-party motor policy
- Article Seven of the 2023 Unified Compulsory Motor Insurance Policy: insurers acknowledge claims and identify missing documentation within **three working days for individuals**; generally settle **individual third-party claims within 15 working days** after receiving **all required documents**; notify claim acceptance/denial within **five working days** of receiving required documents. Corporate claims have separate periods. These are specific to this insurance category, not a universal answer for every comprehensive or financed policy. Source: https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/unified-compulsory-motor-insurance-policy
- For comprehensive insurance, applicable policy and regulatory rules may be different. Example reference (financially leased vehicle rules): https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/article-15-general-provisions
- Do not guarantee the maximum regulation means every claim finishes by that time; missing documents, coverage, exceptions, and claim type matter.

## 4. Possible claim-document topics to confirm against insurer KB
- Accident reference/report issued by the authorized body.
- Authorized damage-assessment report, where required.
- Relevant policy or vehicle details, driving/license documents, and evidence requested by the insurer.
- Securely supplied claimant banking details only when legitimately required for payment, NEVER read or collected through insecure voice interaction.
- Repair invoices if the applicable process calls for reimbursement of repairs. Do not label this an exhaustive or universally required document list.

## 5. Complaints and disputed claims
- In Saudi Arabia, the Insurance Authority (IA) now operates IA Care for insurance-sector complaints, replacing the old SAMA Cares complaint route; check official IA communications for updates. IA Care number: **800 124 0551**; website information: https://www.ia.gov.sa/en/faqs and complaint service https://www.ia.gov.sa/en/Pages/service-details.aspx/Complaint-against-an-insurance-company .
- Escalation should ordinarily begin with the insurer's complaints team, then IA Care or legally appropriate dispute body if unresolved. Do not give case-specific legal advice or claim outcomes.
